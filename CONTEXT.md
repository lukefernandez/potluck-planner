# Glossary

- **Potluck** — a gathering's shared dish list. Created without an account and
  owned by no one; anyone with its link can view and contribute.
- **Item** — a dish someone has committed to bring to a Potluck. Carries the
  bringer's name and dietary flags.
- **Dietary flags** — the heads-up markers on an Item (meat, fish, shellfish,
  eggs, dairy, other animal products, nuts, gluten). They warn guests; they do
  not filter or restrict anything.
- **Dietary badges** — the labels guests see on an Item, derived from its
  dietary flags: one diet badge at most (Vegan, else Vegetarian, else
  Pescatarian), plus stacking free-from badges (dairy, eggs, nuts, gluten).
- **Slug** — the unguessable identifier in a Potluck's shareable link. Knowing
  the slug is the only credential for viewing or contributing.
- **Unlisted page** — a Potluck's page: reachable only by link and excluded
  from search indexes, because it shows guests' names.
- **Warmer** — a scheduled request that keeps the API responsive between
  visits.
